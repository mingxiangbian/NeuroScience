# Error Log Template

| Date | Skill | Error | Category | Source | Impact | Status | Review date |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

## Status Values

- active
- improving
- fixed once
- fixed repeatedly
- regressed

## Categories

- grammar
- collocation
- lexical limitation
- paraphrase failure
- coherence breakdown
- spelling or morphology
- timing
- task response
- pronunciation or fluency

## Review Rule

An error should stay in review until it remains fixed in at least two relevant practice contexts.
