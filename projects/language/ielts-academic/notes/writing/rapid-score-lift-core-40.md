# IELTS Academic 写作临考核心 40 速查页

这张表只用于主动回忆；完整解释、题型解码、主题扩展、Task 1 规则和错误修复请查阅完整版。

## 使用规则

1. 看中文功能或结构，遮住英文完成回忆。
2. 每个句架至少替换两个不同主题，不背固定整句。
3. 连续两次能够在 5 秒内写出，才算进入主动词汇。
4. 每个主体段最多主动调用 2—3 个句架，其余使用普通主谓结构。

# 临考核心 40 单元

## Task 2 功能句架：16

1. `I largely agree that ... / I disagree with this view, mainly because ...`
2. `While I accept that ..., I believe that ...`
3. `I consider this a largely positive / negative development because ...`
4. `On balance, the advantages outweigh the disadvantages because ...`
5. `One important reason is that ...`
6. `This is largely because ...`
7. `When ..., people / institutions are more likely to ...`
8. `By + -ing ..., people / governments can ...`
9. `Without ..., people may struggle to ...`
10. `This can lead to + noun phrase.`
11. `This, in turn, makes it more likely that ...`
12. `This matters because ...`
13. `Consider, for example, a ... who ...`
14. `Admittedly, ...; however, ...`
15. `A more effective approach would be to ...`
16. `In conclusion, I believe that ... because ...`

## Task 2 通用搭配：10

1. `broaden access to ... / reduce financial barriers`
2. `improve learning outcomes`
3. `develop practical and transferable skills`
4. `improve employment prospects`
5. `boost productivity`
6. `protect data privacy`
7. `maintain human oversight`
8. `reduce social inequality / place pressure on public services`
9. `reduce carbon emissions`
10. `improve mental well-being`

## Task 1 稳定表达：14

1. `The chart compares ... in terms of ...`
2. `Overall, ...`
3. `The most striking feature is that ...`
4. `rose from A to B`
5. `fell by A / fell to B`
6. `reached a peak of + figure`
7. `remained broadly stable`
8. `stood at + figure`
9. `accounted for + percentage / share`
10. `was higher / lower than ...`
11. `was twice the figure for ...`
12. `fell / rose by ... percentage points`
13. `was replaced by ... / was converted into ...`
14. `The process begins with ... / ..., after which ...`

# 15 分钟快速训练

## 5 分钟：Task 2 句架

随机选择一题，只写立场、两个主体句和一个让步句，不写全文。

## 5 分钟：Task 2 搭配

从 10 个搭配中任选 5 个，各补一个宾语或主语，例如：

- `broaden access to higher education`
- `place pressure on local hospitals`

## 5 分钟：Task 1 转换

口头完成以下区分：

- `rose by` 与 `rose to`
- 百分比与 percentage points
- `twice the figure for` 与错误的 `twice higher than`
- `was replaced by` 与 `was converted into`
- `The process begins with` 与 `..., after which ...`

# 最后六项检查

- 是否回答所有问号和限定词？
- Task 2 立场是否从开头到结尾一致？
- 每个主体段是否有机制，而非只列结果？
- Task 1 是否有清楚 Overview 和合理分组？
- 数字、对象、单位、方向是否对应？
- 是否为了“高级”使用了不确定搭配？
