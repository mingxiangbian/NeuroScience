# IELTS Academic 写作快速提分与分层表达库（完善版）

## 这份资料解决什么问题

这不是押题表，也不是范文模板库。它的目标是让你在陌生题目中完成四件事：

1. 准确识别题目要求，不漏答、不读反。
2. 用清楚的因果链展开观点，而不是只列结论。
3. 调用准确、可迁移的搭配，不因追求“高级”而制造错误。
4. 在 Task 1 中选择、分组和准确报告主要特征。

官方评分并不奖励孤立的难词。Band 7 更依赖以下组合：任务得到回应、观点或主要特征得到展开、信息有清楚推进、搭配有一定灵活性，并且经常出现无错误句。

> **本版原则：先保证任务完成和逻辑，再增加表达变化。**

## 本版修复了什么

| 原版缺口 | 本版处理 |
| --- | --- |
| Task 2 逻辑句架集中在立场、因果和例证，开头、限定、权衡、结论及多问任务覆盖不足 | 增加“题目解码表”和按功能组织的句架，明确 `outweigh`、原因/方案、多问任务等差异 |
| 主题库以抽象名词为主，知道中文意思却不一定会放进句子 | 改为“动词 + 搭配 + 可替换槽位”，优先保留可以直接进入论证的组合 |
| 主题分布不均，家庭、人口、住房、犯罪、旅游、广告和消费等覆盖偏弱 | 新增相应模块；低频主题仍保留最低必要覆盖，不再机械地按题频分配词条 |
| 缺少跨题生成观点的“机制词” | 新增成本、可及性、激励、反馈、监督、外部成本等通用机制库 |
| Task 1 偏重基础趋势，静态图、倍数、近似值、差距、地图位置和流程衔接不够 | 按动态数据、静态比较、地图、流程和混合图重新补齐 |
| 原版临考计划绑定具体日期，复用性较弱 | 改为考前 48 小时、24 小时和考场三个阶段 |
| 表达数量有明确总数，但不同功能并不天然需要相同数量 | 完整库不再为“凑数”删减；另设一个可控的临考核心层 |

## 如何使用：三层而不是全背

- `★`：核心表达。考前要能不看资料完成替换。
- 无标记：半主动表达。看见能理解，需要时能查阅。
- `⚠`：高风险点。重点不是背，而是避免误用。

完整库用于查阅；临考只激活文末的 **40 个核心单元**。一个“单元”可以是一对必须一起区分的形式，例如 `fell by / fell to`。

### 评分权重对应的复习比例

Task 2 在写作总分中的权重高于 Task 1，因此临考核心按大约 **2:1** 分配：

| 模块 | 临考核心单元 | 目的 |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Task 2 功能句架 | 16 | 覆盖主要题目动作、展开、例证、方案和结论 |
| Task 2 通用搭配 | 10 | 降低主题陌生时的词汇停顿 |
| Task 1 稳定表达 | 14 | 覆盖图表、地图和流程的最低需要 |
| 合计 | 40 | 其余词条只作扩展库 |

| 当前风险 | 考场动作 | 完成标准 |
| --- | --- | --- |

| E010：想不到例子 | 使用“角色 → 行为或限制 → 直接结果 → 回扣观点”的假设场景 | 例子能观察到过程，不需要名人或虚构统计 |

# Task 2

## 一、先解码题目，不要先套题型

题型名称只是检查工具。真正要做的是识别题目中的动作词和必须回答的部分。

| 题目动作 | 必须完成 | 安全立场形式 | 常见失分 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `To what extent do you agree or disagree?` | 明确同意程度，并用全文支持 | `I agree to a large extent because A and B.` | 写成正反各一段，却没有清楚程度 |
| `Discuss both views and give your own opinion.` | 解释两方为什么合理，并给出自己的判断 | `While X may..., I believe Y is more important because...` | 只罗列两方；个人观点只在结尾出现 |
| `What are the advantages and disadvantages?` | 展开至少一个主要优点和一个主要缺点 | 不必判断谁更大，除非题目要求 | 自行加入 `outweigh` 判断，导致偏题 |
| `Do the advantages outweigh the disadvantages?` | 两边都讨论，并明确比较结果 | `On balance, the advantages outweigh the disadvantages because...` | 只写占优的一边，或写两边但不权衡 |
| `Is this a positive or negative development?` | 给总体评价，并说明评价标准 | `I consider this largely positive because...` | 正负都写，却不作最终判断 |
| `Why / What causes ...? What effects / solutions ...?` | 每个问号都给独立答案和展开 | `The main cause is A, while the most effective response is B.` | 第二问只在结论中提一句 |
| 两个或更多直接问题 | 逐项回答，不能假设它们属于同一逻辑 | 在草稿写 `Q1 answer / Q2 answer` | 花太多篇幅回答第一问，第二问展开不足 |

### 命题范围检查

除动作词外，再圈出范围词：

- 人群：children、employees、governments、individuals、society。
- 比较对象：public transport 与 private cars，family 与 government。
- 时间：now、in the future、in the past。
- 程度：all、only、mainly、best、more、most。

正文中的每个理由都必须同时符合“动作”和“范围”。

## 二、五分钟承诺法（完善版）

### 第 1 分钟：写出任务

> Proposition: ________
>
> Command words: ________
>
> Scope / qualifiers: ________
>
> Required answers: Q1 ________ / Q2 ________

### 第 2 分钟：给直接答案

> My answer: ________ because A ________ and B ________.

检查 A、B 是否真的支持这句话。正文开始后不再重新选择立场。

### 第 3—4 分钟：建立两个论证链

每段只记五个词组：

> Claim → Mechanism → Actor → Immediate result → Link

对于 `outweigh` 或 positive/negative 题，再补一个判断标准：

> range / intensity / duration / reversibility

即：影响到多少人、影响多强、持续多久、是否容易补救。

### 第 5 分钟：可写性检查

每个主体段必须同时满足：

1. 直接回答题目。
2. 与另一段不重复。
3. 能用简单英语解释机制。
4. 能形成具体场景。
5. 不依赖未经证实的统计或研究。

满足后立即开始写，不再寻找“更完美”的观点。

## 三、四段式只是安全默认，不是死模板

### Introduction：两项任务

1. 用自己的话界定问题。
2. 直接给出答案或立场。

不要写与题目无关的宏大背景，例如 `With the rapid development of society...`。 题目中的准确核心词可以重复，不必为每个词寻找同义替换。

### Body paragraph：一个段落只承担一个中心主张

1. **Claim**：本段主张。
2. **Mechanism**：为什么会发生。
3. **Concrete scenario**：谁在什么情境下做了什么。
4. **Immediate result**：直接、可观察的结果。
5. **Link / evaluation**：结果如何证明主张，必要时与另一影响比较。

示例：

> One reason is that practical activities provide immediate feedback. This matters because children are more likely to remain engaged when they can see the result of their effort. Consider, for example, a pupil who directs a short classroom play. The pupil can observe how planning and cooperation improve the final performance, which may strengthen the motivation to participate in later lessons.

### Conclusion：两项任务

1. 压缩重申最终答案。
2. 不加入新理由、新例子或新立场。

## 四、按功能组织的高复用句架

### A. 界定问题与改写题目

1. `The question is whether ...`
2. `There is ongoing debate about whether ...`
3. `The issue concerns the extent to which ...`
4. `This issue raises two related questions: why ..., and how / whether ...`

这些句子只能用于准确界定题目；不要为了“高级”把原命题改得更绝对。

### B. 立场与直接答案

1. ★ `I agree to a large extent that ..., mainly because ...`
2. ★ `I disagree with this view, mainly because ...`
3. ★ `While I accept that ..., I believe that ...`
4. `I agree that ...; however, I do not accept that ...`
5. `I consider this a largely positive / negative development because ...`
6. `Whether ... is beneficial depends largely on ...`
7. `In my view, the more important consideration is ...`
8. `On balance, the advantages outweigh the disadvantages because ...`

### C. 提出主张与解释机制

1. ★ `One important reason is that ...`
2. ★ `This is largely because ...`
3. ★ `When ..., people / institutions are more likely to ...`
4. ★ `By + -ing ..., people / schools / governments can ...`
5. ★ `Without ..., people may struggle to ...`
6. `A key factor is the ability to ...`
7. `The underlying reason is that ...`
8. `This creates an incentive for people / firms to ...`
9. `This reduces the time / cost required to ...`
10. `This gives people the opportunity to ...`

### D. 结果、递进与回扣

1. ★ `This can lead to + noun / noun phrase.`
2. ★ `This, in turn, makes it more likely that ...`
3. `As a result, ...`
4. `The immediate consequence is that ...`
5. ★ `This matters because ...`
6. `Over time, this can result in ...`
7. `The broader effect is a reduction / increase in ...`
8. `This is why ... can have a lasting impact on ...`

### E. 例证与支持

1. ★ `Consider, for example, a student / employee / family who ...`
2. `For instance, when ..., ...`
3. `A typical example would be ...`
4. `This is particularly evident in cases where ...`
5. `A simple illustration is a local authority that ...`

例证的目标是显示机制，不是显示知识量。

### F. 限定、概率与条件

1. ★ `... tend(s) to ...`
2. ★ `... is / are more likely to ...`
3. `In many cases, ...`
4. `To a certain extent, ...`
5. `This is especially true when ...`
6. `This outcome is more likely if ...`
7. `Provided that ..., ...`
8. `Unless ..., ... may ...`

限定词可以减少过度概括，但不要在每句都加入 `may`、`might` 和 `can`。

### G. 让步、比较与权衡

1. ★ `Admittedly, ...; however, ...`
2. ★ `Although ... can ..., this does not necessarily mean that ...`
3. `This benefit is limited in cases where ...`
4. `Compared with ..., ... has a broader / longer-lasting impact on ...`
5. ★ `The decisive consideration is whether ...`
6. `The main difference is that ..., whereas ...`
7. `The short-term cost may be ..., but the long-term benefit is ...`
8. `This disadvantage is relatively easy / difficult to reverse because ...`
9. `The effect on ... is likely to be greater than the effect on ...`
10. `On balance, ...`

### H. 原因、问题与方案

1. `One major cause is ...`
2. `The problem is made worse by ...`
3. `A lack of ... can prevent people from ...`
4. ★ `Governments could address this by + -ing ...`
5. ★ `A more effective approach would be to ...`
6. `This measure would be most effective if ...`
7. `Rather than ..., policymakers should ...`
8. `The aim should be to remove the underlying cause, rather than merely to ...`

### I. 结论

1. `In conclusion, I believe that ... because ...`
2. `Overall, although ..., ...`
3. `For these reasons, ...`
4. `On balance, the stronger case is that ...`

结论可以重复核心词。清楚优先于强行换词。

## 五、指代与替代：原版容易被忽略的 Band 7 能力

连接不只依靠 `however` 和 `therefore`。下面这些名词可以把前一句的内容压缩为后一句的主语：

| 指代形式 | 适用对象 | 示例 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `this approach` | 前文的一种做法 | `This approach would reduce the cost of participation.` |
| `this policy / measure` | 政策或干预 | `Such measures are most effective when enforcement is consistent.` |
| `this trend` | 社会或数据趋势 | `This trend may place pressure on public services.` |
| `this change / development` | 变化或发展 | `This development is likely to benefit rural communities.` |
| `these costs / benefits` | 前文的多项影响 | `These costs are borne mainly by low-income families.` |
| `the resulting pressure / gap` | 前文产生的结果 | `The resulting pressure may reduce service quality.` |
| `the same principle` | 可迁移的机制 | `The same principle applies to workplace training.` |

⚠ 指代词必须有唯一、清楚的对象。连续写多个 `this`，却让读者无法判断所指内容，会削弱衔接。

## 六、跨主题“机制库”：先想机制，再找词

| 机制 | 可直接使用的搭配 | 典型因果链 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ★ 可及性 | `broaden access to ...` / `make ... more accessible` | 更多人能够使用 → 参与率上升 → 差距缩小 |
| ★ 成本与负担 | `reduce financial barriers` / `ease the financial burden on ...` | 成本下降 → 低收入群体更能参与 |
| ★ 激励 | `provide an incentive to ...` / `discourage harmful behaviour` | 回报或代价改变 → 行为改变 |
| 时间与便利 | `save time` / `reduce the time required to ...` | 使用门槛下降 → 采用率提高 |
| 信息与认知 | `make informed decisions` / `raise public awareness of ...` | 信息更准确 → 决策质量提高 |
| ★ 技能与反馈 | `develop practical skills` / `receive immediate feedback` | 练习与反馈 → 能力或动机提升 |
| ★ 效率与生产率 | `improve operational efficiency` / `boost productivity` | 相同资源完成更多任务 → 成本下降 |
| ★ 分配与不平等 | `reduce social inequality` / `distribute resources more fairly` | 资源差距缩小 → 机会更平等 |
| 健康与福祉 | `improve mental well-being` / `reduce health risks` | 压力或风险下降 → 生活质量提高 |
| 外部成本 | `impose costs on ...` / `place pressure on ...` | 个体行为把成本转移给社会或环境 |
| ★ 监督与责任 | `maintain human oversight` / `hold ... accountable for ...` | 决策可检查 → 错误和滥用减少 |
| 社会联系 | `strengthen social cohesion` / `foster a sense of belonging` | 互动与信任增加 → 合作和支持增强 |
| 长期习惯 | `promote long-term behavioural change` / `form lasting habits` | 重复行为 → 稳定习惯 → 长期效果 |
| 创新与适应 | `encourage innovation` / `adapt to changing demand` | 试验和竞争 → 新方案出现 |

### 机制选择规则

一个主体段通常只需要一个主机制。不要在同一段同时写“省钱、提高健康、促进平等、保护环境”，否则每个点都只能停留在口号层面。

## 七、平衡后的高频主题搭配

以下词条是学习设计，不是官方词表。优先记“动词 + 名词”的整体，不要只背名词。

### 1. 教育与青少年

- ★ `improve academic performance`：提高学业表现
- ★ `lead to better learning outcomes`：带来更好的学习成果
- ★ `foster intrinsic motivation`：培养内在动机
- ★ `develop practical and transferable skills`：培养实践及可迁移技能
- `provide equal educational opportunities`：提供平等教育机会
- `maintain a balanced curriculum`：保持均衡课程体系
- `reduce academic pressure on students`：减轻学生学业压力
- `prepare students for the workplace`：帮助学生为职场做准备
- `encourage independent learning`：鼓励自主学习
- `receive constructive feedback`：获得建设性反馈

### 2. 工作与经济

- ★ `improve employment prospects`：改善就业前景
- `meet labour-market demand`：满足劳动力市场需求
- `gain relevant workplace experience`：获得相关职场经验
- ★ `boost productivity`：提高生产率
- `create job opportunities`：创造就业机会
- `provide financial security`：提供经济保障
- `maintain a healthy work-life balance`：保持健康的工作生活平衡
- `support small businesses`：支持小型企业
- `stimulate economic growth`：促进经济增长
- `adapt to changing labour-market needs`：适应变化的劳动力需求

### 3. 科技与科学

- ★ `broaden access to information`：扩大信息获取渠道
- `automate routine tasks`：自动化重复性工作
- `improve operational efficiency`：提高运行效率
- ★ `protect data privacy`：保护数据隐私
- `bridge the digital divide`：缩小数字鸿沟
- ★ `maintain human oversight`：保留人工监督
- `promote scientific innovation`：促进科学创新
- `reduce human error`：减少人为错误
- `spread misinformation at scale`：大规模传播错误信息
- `rely too heavily on automated systems`：过度依赖自动化系统

### 4. 政府、社会与不平等

- `strengthen social cohesion`：增强社会凝聚力
- `foster a sense of belonging`：培养归属感
- `preserve individual autonomy`：维护个人自主性
- `promote long-term behavioural change`：促进长期行为改变
- `ensure equal access to essential services`：确保平等获得基本服务
- ★ `reduce social inequality`：减少社会不平等
- ★ `place pressure on public services`：给公共服务造成压力
- `protect vulnerable groups`：保护弱势群体
- `allocate public funds efficiently`：高效配置公共资金
- `strike a balance between individual freedom and the public interest`：平衡个人自由与公共利益

### 5. 环境、交通、城市与住房

- ★ `reduce carbon emissions`：减少碳排放
- `invest in public transport infrastructure`：投资公共交通基础设施
- `ease urban congestion`：缓解城市拥堵
- `encourage sustainable consumption`：鼓励可持续消费
- `conserve natural resources`：节约自然资源
- `improve air quality`：改善空气质量
- `manage urban expansion`：管理城市扩张
- `provide affordable housing`：提供可负担住房
- `reduce dependence on private cars`：降低对私家车的依赖
- `protect natural habitats`：保护自然栖息地

### 6. 健康与生活方式

- `invest in preventive healthcare`：投资预防性医疗
- ★ `improve mental well-being`：改善心理健康状态
- `discourage a sedentary lifestyle`：减少久坐生活方式
- `improve access to medical care`：改善医疗可及性
- `reduce the burden on the healthcare system`：减轻医疗系统负担
- `encourage regular physical activity`：鼓励规律运动
- `adopt a balanced diet`：采用均衡饮食
- `address public health risks`：应对公共健康风险
- `receive timely treatment`：及时接受治疗

### 7. 家庭、儿童与人口

- `provide effective parental guidance`：提供有效的家长引导
- `support healthy child development`：支持儿童健康发展
- `strengthen family relationships`：加强家庭关系
- `share childcare responsibilities`：分担育儿责任
- `respond to an ageing population`：应对人口老龄化
- `provide adequate care for older people`：提供充分养老照护
- `improve intergenerational communication`：改善代际沟通
- `delay starting a family`：推迟组建家庭
- `balance work and family commitments`：平衡工作与家庭责任

### 8. 文化、媒体、旅游、全球化与消费

- `preserve cultural heritage`：保护文化遗产
- `promote cultural diversity`：促进文化多样性
- `improve media literacy`：提高媒介素养
- `protect local traditions`：保护本地传统
- `facilitate cultural exchange`：促进文化交流
- `generate tourism revenue`：创造旅游收入
- `disrupt local communities`：扰乱当地社区
- `influence consumer behaviour`：影响消费者行为
- `encourage impulse purchases`：刺激冲动消费
- `use misleading advertising`：使用误导性广告
- `expose local firms to global competition`：使本地企业面对全球竞争
- `make cultural preferences more uniform`：使文化偏好趋同

### 9. 犯罪、法律与公共安全

- `deter criminal behaviour`：震慑犯罪行为
- `maintain public safety`：维护公共安全
- `enforce the law consistently`：持续一致地执法
- `rehabilitate offenders`：改造违法者
- `address the root causes of crime`：处理犯罪根源
- `reduce reoffending rates`：降低再犯率
- `impose proportionate penalties`：施加与行为相称的处罚
- `strengthen community policing`：加强社区警务

| --- | --- | --- |

### 例证生成式

> actor → action or constraint → mechanism → immediate result → connection to the claim

例如：

> Consider a low-income student who receives a transport subsidy. By reducing the daily cost of travelling to college, the policy makes regular attendance more affordable. The student is therefore less likely to miss classes, which can improve learning outcomes over time.

## 九、Task 2 语法与搭配安全区

| 功能 | 正确形式 | 常见错误 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 原因 | `because + clause` / `because of + noun phrase` | `because of people cannot...` |
| 结果 | `lead to / result in + noun phrase` | `lead to people can...` |
| 从句结果 | `mean that + clause` | `lead to the result that...` |
| 使某人能够 | `allow / enable somebody to do` | `enable somebody doing` |
| 阻止 | `prevent somebody from doing` | `prevent somebody to do` |
| 提供 | `provide somebody with something` / `provide something for somebody` | `provide somebody something` |
| 介词搭配 | `access to` / `demand for` / `pressure on` / `impact on` / `solution to` | `access of` / `pressure to services` |
| 支出 | `spend money on` / `invest money in` | `spend money in education` |
| 评价 | `be beneficial to` / `be convenient for` | `people are convenient` |
| 讨论 | `discuss something` | `discuss about something` |
| 社会 | `society`（泛指） | `the society`（无特定社会时） |
| 生活 | `people's lives` | `people's life`（泛指多人） |
| 不可数名词 | `information` / `equipment` / `research` | `informations` / `equipments` / `researches` |

### 六种够用的复杂句

1. `Although A, B.`
2. `When A, B.`
3. `If A, B.`
4. `People who ..., ...`
5. `By + -ing ..., ...`
6. `A, which ..., ...`

复杂句不是越长越好。先保证主语、谓语和从句边界清楚。

### 不建议主动使用的套话

- `With the rapid development of society, ...`
- `Every coin has two sides.`
- `It is universally acknowledged that ...`
- `There is no doubt that ...`
- `According to research, ...`（没有真实来源）
- `This essay will discuss both sides before giving my opinion.`（不是语法错误，但通常浪费字数且没有直接回答）

# Task 1

## 一、先判断视觉类型和语言任务

| 类型 | 首要任务 | 常用时态 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 有时间变化的线图、柱图、表格或饼图 | 找总体方向、峰值、交叉、差距变化和排名变化 | 已结束时期用过去时；延伸到现在可混合；预测用将来或预测表达 |
| 无时间变化的静态柱图、表格或饼图 | 找最大/最小、相似组、显著差距和倍数关系 | 通常一般过去时或一般现在时，取决于题目时间 |
| Mixed chart | 找两图共同讲述的主线，再决定如何交叉分组 | 由每个图的时间决定 |
| Map | 找用途、布局、密度、交通和保留区域的整体变化 | 过去对比用过去时；规划图常用将来或被动形式 |
| Process diagram | 找起点、终点、阶段数、是否循环以及人工/自然过程 | 通常一般现在时；人工过程常使用被动语态 |

## 二、20 分钟分配

| 时间 | 动作 |
| --- | --- |
| 0—3 分钟 | 看标题、单位、年份、对象和图型；圈出 2—3 个主要特征；确定分组 |
| 3—5 分钟 | 写 Introduction 和 Overview |
| 5—16 分钟 | 写两个细节段，只选择支持主要特征的数据 |
| 16—20 分钟 | 核对对象、年份、数字、单位、方向、最高最低、主谓一致和时态 |

## 三、Introduction：按图型改写

1. ★ `The line graph illustrates changes in ... between A and B.`
2. ★ `The bar chart compares ... in terms of ...`
3. `The table provides information about ...`
4. `The pie charts show how ... is / was distributed among ...`
5. `The diagrams show how ... changed between A and B.`
6. `The diagram illustrates the stages involved in ...`
7. `The two charts compare ... and provide information about ...`

Introduction 只说明图表展示什么，不写主要结论。

## 四、Overview：按图型选主要特征

### 动态数据图

依次检查：

1. 总体上升、下降、稳定还是方向不同？
2. 谁始终最高或最低？
3. 是否有交叉、超越或排名变化？
4. 哪个变化幅度最大、最晚或最反常？
5. 差距是扩大、缩小还是基本不变？

可用表达：

- ★ `Overall, X increased, whereas Y declined.`
- ★ `Overall, X remained the largest category throughout the period.`
- ★ `The most striking feature is that ...`
- `Overall, the gap between X and Y narrowed / widened considerably.`
- `Overall, X overtook Y, while Z remained broadly stable.`

### 静态柱图、表格和饼图

检查：

1. 最大和最小类别。
2. 哪些对象数值接近，可以成组。
3. 是否存在明显倍数或异常值。
4. 总量和构成是否讲述同一结论。

可用表达：

- `Overall, X accounted for the largest share, while Y accounted for the smallest.`
- `Overall, the figures for A and B were similar and substantially higher than those for C and D.`
- `The most notable contrast was between X and Y.`

### Mixed chart

不要把两张图各写成一篇小作文。先找共同主线：

- `Taken together, the two charts show that ...`
- `The pattern in X was broadly consistent with that in Y.`
- `Despite the increase in X, Y remained ...`

### Map

Overview 优先写：用途变化、建筑密度、道路/步行可达性和保留区域。

- `Overall, the area became more residential / commercial / accessible.`
- `Overall, the site was substantially redeveloped, although ... remained unchanged.`
- `The most notable changes were the addition of ... and the removal of ...`

### Process

Overview 优先写：线性或循环、起点、终点、主要阶段和显著转化。

- `Overall, this is a linear process consisting of X main stages, beginning with ... and ending with ...`
- `Overall, the process is cyclical, as the final stage feeds back into the first.`
- `Overall, raw materials pass through X main stages before the final product is produced.`

Overview 通常不塞入大量数字；只有在某个数字本身就是核心特征时才保留。

## 五、动态趋势表达

### 上升和下降

- ★ `rose / increased from A to B`
- ★ `fell / declined from A to B`
- `grew / declined steadily`
- `rose / fell sharply`
- `increased / decreased gradually`
- `recorded a slight increase / decrease`

⚠ 程度副词必须由图形支持。不要因为想变化词汇而随意写 `dramatically`。

### 峰值、低点和转折

- ★ `reached a peak of + figure`
- `peaked at + figure`
- `fell to a low of + figure`
- `rose to ..., before falling to ...`
- `declined initially, then recovered to ...`
- `overtook / surpassed X in + year`

`fluctuated` 通常表示多次上下变化。只有一次“先升后降”时，优先直接描述两个动作。

### 稳定、重复和幅度

- ★ `remained broadly stable at around ...`
- `showed little change over the period`
- `fluctuated between A and B`
- `doubled / tripled over the period`
- `fell by half`
- `ended the period at ...`

## 六、静态比较和数据报告

### 基础报告

- ★ `stood at + figure`
- ★ `accounted for + percentage / share`
- `represented / made up + percentage`（确认会用再使用）
- `was the largest / smallest category`
- `the figure for X`
- `the corresponding figure for Y`

### 高低、差距和相似

- ★ `was higher / lower than ...`
- `was similar to / almost identical to ...`
- `exceeded X by + figure`
- `was X units / percentage points higher than ...`
- `there was a gap of + figure between A and B`
- `A and B recorded comparable figures`
- `the combined figure for A and B`

### 倍数

- ★ `was twice the figure for X`
- `was three times the figure for X`
- `was approximately half the figure for X`
- `there were twice as many X as Y`
- `the proportion of A was twice that of B`

⚠ 避免 `twice higher than` 和 `three times more than`。优先使用 `twice / three times the figure for`。

### 近似值

- `approximately / roughly + figure`
- `around + figure`
- `just over / just under + figure`
- `nearly / almost + figure`

不要把两个近似词叠加，例如 `around approximately 50`。

### `respectively` 的安全规则

仅在两组项目数量相同、顺序一一对应时使用：

> `France and Germany recorded 40% and 30%, respectively.`

如果读者需要回头猜对应关系，就拆成两句。

## 七、`by`、`to`、百分比和百分点

| 形式 | 含义 | 示例 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `rose to 50%` | 终点是 50% | 20% → 50% |
| `rose by 30 percentage points` | 增加了 30 个百分点 | 20% → 50% |
| `rose by 150%` | 相对原值增加 150% | 20 → 50 |
| `fell to 19%` | 终点是 19% | 76% → 19% |
| `fell by 57 percentage points` | 百分比之差为 57 个百分点 | 76% → 19% |

考试中若不需要计算相对变化，优先报告起点和终点，错误风险更低。

## 八、分组和衔接

- `Turning to X, ...`
- `By contrast, ...`
- `Similarly, ...`
- `In comparison, ...`
- `While X stood at A, Y was only B.`
- `Both X and Y ...`
- `The same pattern can be seen in ...`
- `The corresponding figure for ... was ...`

一个细节段通常围绕一个分组原则：同方向、同水平、同阶段或同区域。不要按图例顺序机械抄写。

## 九、地图表达

### 建造、移除和改造

- ★ `was built / constructed`
- `was demolished / removed`
- ★ `was replaced by ...`
- ★ `was converted into ...`
- `was expanded / extended`
- `was relocated to ...`
- `was added to the site`
- `was pedestrianised`
- `remained unchanged`
- `access to ... was improved by ...`

### 位置

- `in the centre of ...`
- `in the north-western corner of ...`
- `along the eastern side of ...`
- `adjacent to ...`
- `opposite ...`
- `at the entrance to ...`
- `to the north / south of ...`

### 整体变化

- `The area became more densely developed.`
- `The site shifted from industrial use to residential use.`
- `Road access was improved, while more pedestrian space was created.`

## 十、流程图表达

- ★ `The process begins with ...`
- `The process consists of X main stages.`
- ★ `X is then transported / heated / separated ...`
- ★ `..., after which ...`
- `Once X has been ..., it is ...`
- `X is ... before being ...`
- `The material passes through ...`
- `The resulting product is then ...`
- `At the final stage, ...`
- `The process ends with ...`
- `The cycle then repeats.`

⚠ 流程图写“发生了什么”，不要补充图中没有提供的原因、温度、时长或评价。

## 十一、Task 1 名词、时态和主谓安全区

| 需要表达 | 安全形式 | 说明 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 可数对象数量 | `the number of + plural countable noun` | `the number of students was ...` |
| 不可数对象数量 | `the amount of + uncountable noun` | 如 `waste`、`water`；能直接用单位时更简洁 |
| 百分比统计 | `the percentage / proportion of + noun` | `The percentage of students was 40%.` 此时核心名词为单数 |
| 百分数作主语 | `X% of + noun` | 谓语与后面的名词一致：`40% of students were ...`；`40% of waste was ...` |
| 某对象数据 | `the figure for + noun` | 避免反复改写对象 |
| 图表主语 | `The chart shows / compares ...` | 不必纠结 `data is/are` |
| 过去数据 | `was / rose / fell` | 年份已经结束 |
| 流程 | 一般现在时，常用被动语态 | `is transported` |
| 预测 | `is expected / projected to ...` | 只有图中明确为预测时使用 |

## 十二、针对现有及常见错误的安全改写

| 不稳定表达 | 稳定表达 |
| --- | --- |
| `the jobs account in healthcare rank first` | `The number of jobs in healthcare was the highest.` |
| `full-time undergraduates ranked first` | `Full-time undergraduates accounted for the largest proportion of users.` |
| `almost 100 times than the 1800 figure` | `almost 100 times the figure for 1800` |
| `the share declined by 57%`（76% 到 19%） | `the share fell by 57 percentage points` |
| `fluctuated`（只有一次先升后降） | `rose to a peak of ..., before falling to ...` |
| `leads to the result that + clause` | `means that + clause` 或 `leads to + noun phrase` |
| `the amount of students` | `the number of students` |
| `there were 20% people ...` | `20% of people ...` |
| `X had 30%` | `X accounted for 30%.` |
| `twice higher than X` | `twice the figure for X` |
| `compared with A, B was ...`（对象不平行） | `The figure for B was ..., compared with ... for A.` |
| `at around approximately 50` | `at around 50` 或 `at approximately 50` |
| `X increased 20% to 50%` | `X rose from 20% to 50%.` |
| `A and B were 20 and 30 respectively`（标点和单位不清） | `A and B stood at 20% and 30%, respectively.` |

## 十三、Task 1 禁止事项

- 不为了避免重复而错误替换 `number`、`proportion`、`population`、`employment`。
- 不使用不确定的趋势副词或“高级同义词”。
- 不把每一个数据都写进正文。
- 不在 Overview 中加入原因、评价或个人观点。
- 不把 `percentage`、百分比相对变化和 `percentage points` 混用。
- 不用 `rank first` 描述数量，优先用 `was the highest` 或 `accounted for the largest share`。
- 不把地图写成参观路线，也不把流程写成建议说明。
- Task 1 不需要单独的 Conclusion。

# 考前 48 小时的使用方法

## T − 48 小时

1. 对 40 个核心单元做中译英或填空式回忆；不会的单独标记，不抄写整页。
2. 对两道陌生 Task 2 完成五分钟提纲，每段写出机制和例证链。
3. 分别对动态图、静态图、地图和流程图各写一个 Overview。
4. 完成 10 组安全转换：`because / because of`、`lead to / mean that`、`by / to`、百分比/百分点、倍数。

## T − 24 小时

1. 完成一次 60 分钟写作：Task 2 约 40 分钟，Task 1 约 20 分钟。
2. 不逐句追求完美，只统计六类高影响问题：漏答、立场反向、论点未展开、搭配错误、数字错误、检查遗漏。
3. 只重写最差的一个 Task 2 主体段和一个 Task 1 Overview。
4. 睡前只复习 40 个核心单元，不再扩充词表。

## 考场

- Task 2 开始前执行五分钟承诺法。
- 每个主体段只承担一个中心主张和一个主要机制。
- 例子使用普通角色和具体场景，不等待名人、新闻或统计数字。
- Task 1 先确定分组，再写细节；允许准确重复核心统计名词。
- 最后检查优先级：漏答和立场 → 主语谓语 → 数据与单位 → 单复数和拼写 → 可删的多余词。
