# IELTS Speaking Part 2 可复习资料库

这是 Part 2 的成品复习入口，不是素材收集台账。完整原题及中英翻译见 [2026 年 5-8 月 Part 2 题库与素材分类](./2026-may-august-part-2-topic-clusters.md)，详细事实来源见 [Part 2 Knowledge Bank](./part-2-knowledge-bank.md)。

- 覆盖全部 `55` 道题。
- `54` 道题使用真实经历或经可靠来源核对的材料。
- `PE11` 没有真实儿童素材，保留为明确标记的应急合成卡。
- 所有题目目前均为“内容就绪”，尚未完成两分钟口述验证；不能把内容就绪等同于考试就绪。
- 不背整篇答案。先用中文记忆链恢复事实，再用英文表达块组织自己的答案。

## 复习方法

1. 一分钟准备时，只看“关键词”和对应题目的“切换重点”。
2. 口述时按“背景 - 具体场景 - 结果 - 感受”展开，不要求逐字复现英文表达块。
3. 结束后检查“事实边界”，避免把不同时间线、人物角色或完成状态混在一起。

## R01 - 从等待机会到主动行动

**覆盖：** EV06、EV08、OB03、OB05、OB08、OB13

**中文记忆链：** 大三末以前较被动，认为机会主要靠运气 - 6 月在 Bilibili 看约一小时的姚顺雨访谈 - 他从物理本科走向 AI 研究并主动学习、投递简历 - 意识到行动能创造选择 - 改变 IELTS 学习方式，主动用 ChatGPT 练口语、追问、纠错和整理素材 - 固定日计划两天才能完成，于是改成可顺延的优先级计划。

**一分钟关键词：**

> third year → passive → Shunyu Yao interview → physics to AI → keep learning / send CV → take initiative → ChatGPT → speaking practice → flexible priority plan

**英文表达块：**

> I used to believe that good opportunities depended mainly on luck, so I often waited instead of taking action.

> A one-hour interview with research scientist Shunyu Yao changed my view because he kept learning and applying for opportunities rather than waiting for ideal conditions.

> I then began using ChatGPT actively for speaking practice, follow-up questions, corrections, and organising my Part 2 material.

> When one planned day of IELTS work started taking almost two actual days, I replaced the fixed schedule with a flexible, priority-based plan.

**题目切换：**

- **EV06 重要决定：** 突出“不再等待，主动创造选择”。
- **EV08 改变计划：** 突出固定日程过量、任务可顺延、口语优先。
- **OB03 外语学习工具：** 突出实时口语、解释、纠错和主动提取。
- **OB05 网络节目：** 突出 6 月、Bilibili、约一小时、物理转 AI、AI 未来观点。
- **OB08 有趣视频：** 突出“主动学习和投简历”改变了自己的想法。
- **OB13 程序或应用：** 突出 ChatGPT 在电脑或手机上的个性化学习用途。

**事实边界：** 不编造访谈节目名称；不声称新计划已带来最终分数提升。

## R02 - 四人循迹小车项目

**覆盖：** PE06、PE10、EV03、EV05、EV09、EV13、EV14

**中文记忆链：** 上学期四人团队从零制作循迹避障小车 - 分工为传感器板、控制板/PID、实验记录、自己的 3D 建模打印与组装 - 负责人制定计划、截止时间和任务 - 两块 PCB 无法连接时提出增加中间接口板 - 控制板布局太紧凑导致多次焊接失败，自己用证据说服队友重做 - 电路延迟压缩测试时间，机械间隙和 PID 调参不足，最终测试没有完全成功 - 学到集成和测试不能留到最后。

**一分钟关键词：**

> last term → four people → line-following car → four roles → my 3D print / assembly → interface board → compact PCB → redesign → delay → little PID testing → final test → integration lesson

**英文表达块：**

> We worked in a four-person team to build a small line-following car, and my main responsibility was designing and 3D-printing the structure and assembling the components.

> When the sensor board and control board could not be connected directly, one teammate suggested adding a small interface board, and the solution worked.

> I also persuaded another teammate to redesign a PCB because its layout was too compact and had caused repeated soldering failures.

> The final test did not fully succeed because circuit delays left too little time for mechanical testing and PID tuning, but it taught me the importance of integration.

**题目切换：**

- **PE06 巧妙解决问题的人：** 只讲提出接口板的项目负责人。
- **PE10 经常制定计划的人：** 讲分工、截止时间、四项任务及遇到新问题后调整方案。
- **EV03 很久才回复消息：** 截止前约六天忙于项目，母亲日常问候几天未回，后来道歉并解释。
- **EV05 技术难题：** 讲电路延迟、低底盘间隙、PID 时间不足和集成失败。
- **EV09 团队合作：** 讲四人分工、相互依赖和最终教训。
- **EV13 劝别人做不愿做的事：** 讲队友因沉没成本拒绝重做 PCB，自己用失败证据说服他。
- **EV14 发挥想象力：** 讲没有现成结构，自己建模、打印、排列和组装部件。

**事实边界：** 整体最终测试没有完全成功；接口板和重新设计控制板分别解决了局部问题。不要把两者说成同一位队友或同一问题。

## R03 - Spider-Man 宣传片与朋友观影

**覆盖：** EV01、OB06、OB11

**中文记忆链：** 很久没见朋友 - 都喜欢 Marvel - 6 月在 Bilibili 看到官方宣传片 - Spider-Man 搂着 Messi 荡蛛丝，Messi 表情僵硬，网友调侃演技 - 觉得好笑并更期待电影 - 上映后与朋友约好一起看，自己负责订票 - 看完讨论角色、评分和未来电影 - 借电影重新联系。

**一分钟关键词：**

> long time no see → Marvel fans → June / Bilibili → Messi + Spider-Man → web swinging → stiff expression → funny → booked tickets → cinema → discussion → reconnect

**英文表达块：**

> Since we had not seen each other in ages, we arranged to watch *Spider-Man: Brand New Day* together, and I booked the tickets.

> The funniest promotional scene showed Spider-Man holding Messi while they swung between buildings on a web, and many viewers joked about Messi's stiff expression.

> After the film, we compared our opinions and talked about future Marvel movies, which reminded me how much I had missed spending time with them.

**题目切换：**

- **EV01 组织的开心活动：** 协调观影、负责订票、朋友参加、重新联系。
- **OB06 名人广告：** 只讲 6 月宣传片、Messi、蛛丝摆荡、网友反应和自己的期待。
- **OB11 最近看的电影：** 讲共同兴趣、观影和看完后的讨论。

**事实边界：** Messi 只出现在宣传片中，不能说他出演了电影；不把普通观影编成生日或大型派对。

## R04 - 二手湖：环境变化与低成本露营

**覆盖：** EV02、OB09、PL01、PL02、PL04

**中文记忆链：** 家乡附近的二手湖 - 祖父母过去在那里钓鱼 - 以前脏且有异味 - 政府治理和旅游开发后更干净、更适合散步 - 现在和家人去 - 一次与父母自带露营用品和食材，在湖边草地露营 - 因为离家近，总花费约 10 元 - 希望引入更严格的湖边垃圾罚款和生态保护规则。

**一分钟关键词：**

> hometown lake → grandparents fishing → dirty / smell → cleanup → cleaner → family → parents → own camping gear and food → grass by lake → about 10 yuan → litter fines / ecosystem

**英文表达块：**

> Ershou Lake is close to my hometown, and my grandparents used to fish there.

> It used to be dirty and unpleasant, but environmental cleanup gradually made it cleaner and more attractive.

> On one outing, my parents and I brought our own camping supplies and food and camped on the grass beside the lake, so the whole day cost only about ten yuan.

> I would introduce clearer fines for littering around lakes and stronger rules to protect fish and the wider ecosystem.

**题目切换：**

- **EV02 低成本特别外出：** 父母、自带用品和食材、湖边草地、约 10 元。
- **OB09 希望引入的新规定：** 湖边乱扔垃圾罚款、保护鱼类与生态。
- **PL01 重要湖泊：** 家乡、祖父母、环境变化和公共价值。
- **PL02 推荐的国内地点：** 干净、适合家庭散步和放松。
- **PL04 空闲想去的地方：** 和家人散步、清空思绪、缓解学习压力。

**事实边界：** 不声称露营过夜或步行到湖边；地方治理措施不是已确认名称的法律。

## R05 - 姐姐的两次教育选择

**覆盖：** EV17、EV18

**中文记忆链：** 事件一：姐姐高考后选大学 - 分数限制选项，也有专业偏好 - 自己和母亲查学校、比专业和利弊 - 建议兼顾现实条件与兴趣。事件二：姐姐后来有稳定工作 - 放弃稳定路径，选择攻读真正想读的研究生 - 她承担不确定性的魄力让自己自豪。

**一分钟关键词：**

> EV17: sister → grades → preferred major → mother and me → research → compare → advice → informed choice

> EV18: stable job → leave security → desired postgraduate degree → uncertainty → courage → proud

**英文表达块：**

> My mother and I helped my sister compare universities and majors based on both her grades and her genuine interests.

> I tried not to make the decision for her; I encouraged her to balance realistic options with what she actually wanted to study.

> Later, she gave up a stable job to pursue the postgraduate degree she genuinely wanted, and I was proud of her courage to leave a secure path.

**题目切换：**

- **EV17 给别人建议：** 使用高考后选大学的事件。
- **EV18 为家人自豪：** 使用放弃稳定工作读研究生的事件。

**事实边界：** 两题是不同时间的事件；不声称她已完成研究生学位或取得未确认的结果。

## R06 - 仁和新城：方便但乏味的高层商场

**覆盖：** PL05、PL07、PL08

**中文记忆链：** 家附近的高层商业综合体 - 有服装、咖啡、餐饮、电影院和促销 - 和朋友看电影，和父母喝咖啡 - 每次体验相似、缺少独特活动 - 方便但不令人兴奋 - 高层外观普通且给自己压迫感，让人联想到快节奏与竞争，更喜欢自然、有生活感的独立小房子。

**一分钟关键词：**

> Renhe Xincheng → near home → clothes / coffee / food / cinema → friends / parents → convenient → repetitive → nothing unique → high-rise → oppressive → fast pace / competition

**英文表达块：**

> Renhe Xincheng is a shopping centre near my home with ordinary facilities such as restaurants, coffee shops, clothing stores, and a cinema.

> It is convenient, but every visit feels almost the same and there is nothing particularly distinctive about it.

> I also dislike the oppressive feeling of the high-rise building because it reminds me of fast-paced urban life and competitive pressure.

**题目切换：**

- **PL05 无聊的地方：** 重复、可预测、没有探索欲。
- **PL07 不喜欢的高层建筑：** 压迫感、城市节奏与竞争，和独立小房子对比。
- **PL08 购物中心：** 地点、设施、同行者、日常活动和正反评价。

**事实边界：** 这是个人感受，不能说建筑不安全或设计质量有问题。

## R07 - 从 256 GB 改买 512 GB 手机

**覆盖：** OB10、OB15

**中文记忆链：** 以前用 256 GB 经常删不常用 App - 原计划仍买 256 GB - 购买时临时改成 512 GB - 多花约人民币 2,000 元 - 后来不再担心空间，也不用持续删 App - 认为值得 - 手机还承担联系、学习、地图、支付、拍照和日程等功能。

**一分钟关键词：**

> old 256 GB → delete apps → planned 256 GB → changed mind → 512 GB → 2,000 yuan extra → no storage worry → worth it → daily tasks

**英文表达块：**

> I originally planned to buy the 256-gigabyte version, but I changed my mind at the time of purchase and chose 512 gigabytes instead.

> It cost about two thousand yuan more, but I no longer need to delete less frequently used apps or worry about storage.

> I rely on my phone for communication, study, maps, digital payments, photos, and many small everyday tasks.

**题目切换：**

- **OB10 花费超过预期：** 突出容量选择、约 2,000 元价差和为什么值得。
- **OB15 离不开的东西：** 突出高频用途与日常依赖，不要字面声称无法生存。

**事实边界：** 不把相机、性能、配件或使用寿命说成升级原因。

## R08 - Boston：MIT 暑期项目与 Celtics 比赛

**覆盖：** EV07、EV11、PL06

**中文记忆链：** 去 Boston 参加 MIT 短期暑期项目 - 用 NeRF 将视频重建为 3D 模型，自己负责平滑噪声和伪影 - 最后一天平时 9 点起、当天 7 点起，协调分工并准备 poster，同题组第二名 - 在 TD Garden 看 Celtics NBA 比赛 - 不记得对手 - 进攻时喊 Go Celtics，防守时喊 Defense - 原以为 Boston 只有学术气氛，后来看到充满活力的一面 - 确认它是去过且最喜欢的城市。

**一分钟关键词：**

> Boston → MIT summer project → NeRF → smooth artifacts → final day 7 a.m. → poster → second place → TD Garden → Celtics → chants → academic + energetic → favourite city

**英文表达块：**

> I went to Boston for a short summer project at MIT, where we used NeRF to reconstruct 3D models from video footage.

> On the final day, I got up at seven instead of my usual nine to coordinate the presentation and prepare our poster, and our group placed second among teams working on the same topic.

> At TD Garden, the crowd cheered for the Celtics on offence and repeatedly shouted "Defense!" when the other team had the ball.

> The contrast between the academic project and the energetic sports culture made Boston my favourite city that I have visited.

**题目切换：**

- **EV07 现场体育赛事：** TD Garden、Celtics、观众随攻防切换口号。
- **EV11 早起：** 平时 9 点、当天 7 点、分工、poster、同题组第二。
- **PL06 最喜欢的城市：** MIT 学术印象与球馆活力的反差。

**事实边界：** 不猜比赛对手和比分；不编造 NeRF 平滑算法；“第二名”只是在同题小组中。

## R09 - Jensen Huang 与长期 AI 抱负

**覆盖：** PE02、PE12、OB02、OB14

**中文记忆链：** 第一次给自己的电脑选 GPU 时知道 NVIDIA 和 Jensen Huang - 后来通过科技新闻和 NVIDIA 发布会了解他 - 最钦佩其对 GPU、CUDA 和 AI 计算的长期判断 - 想问他如何提前识别机会并坚持投入 - 童年看 Iron Man 的 JARVIS 产生智能助手想法 - 大学初期变成认真目标：持续的内部情感状态影响记忆、判断和行动 - 已学习模型训练和 Agent 架构，并有未完成的 GitHub 项目。

**一分钟关键词：**

> first computer → choose GPU → NVIDIA → news / keynotes → GPU + CUDA + AI foresight → want to ask → JARVIS → university → persistent internal affective state → memory / judgment / action → ongoing project

**英文表达块：**

> I first learned about Jensen Huang when I was choosing a GPU for my first computer, and I later followed NVIDIA news and keynote presentations.

> What I respect most is his long-term judgment about GPU computing, CUDA, and AI before their full potential became obvious.

> My long-term ambition is to build an AI assistant with a persistent internal affective state that can influence its memory, judgment, and actions over time.

> I have studied model training and agent architecture and started a related GitHub project, but it is still ongoing.

**题目切换：**

- **PE02 钦佩的成功商人：** 长期技术判断，不以市值作为唯一证据。
- **PE12 想见的名人：** 想问如何识别并坚持长期机会。
- **OB02 长期抱负：** JARVIS 启发、大学认真化、功能目标和已做准备。
- **OB14 理想工作：** AI 研发、构建智能系统和研究智能如何产生。

**事实边界：** 不声称认识 Jensen Huang；JARVIS 只是虚构启发；GitHub 项目尚未完成。

## R10 - 外向的童年好友

**覆盖：** PE01、PE05、PE08

**中文记忆链：** 小学认识的好友 - 一起学习、吃饭、逛街 - 他外向，自己更内向 - 英语成绩好，不只依赖课本，也用歌曲和电影学习真实表达 - Oxford 旅行时与领队走散，他向当地老人问路并带大家回酒店 - 自己擅长数学物理，向他展示错题本，记录错题、原因和正确解法 - 他独立建立自己的错题本，成绩提高。

**一分钟关键词：**

> primary-school friend → outgoing vs reserved → English through songs / films → Oxford → separated → asks local person → hotel → my error notebook → mistake + reason + solution → grades improve

**英文表达块：**

> We have known each other since primary school, and his outgoing personality is very different from my more reserved one.

> He learns English through music and films as well as textbooks, and at Oxford he confidently asked a local person for directions when we became separated from our group.

> I showed him how I recorded wrong questions, the reasons for my mistakes, and the correct solutions; he later created his own error notebook and improved his maths and physics grades.

**题目切换：**

- **PE01 擅长新语言：** 歌曲电影学习法与 Oxford 问路。
- **PE05 最喜欢的童年朋友：** 长期关系、性格反差和共同经历。
- **PE08 从别人学技能的朋友：** 他向自己学错题本方法，角色不能颠倒。

**事实边界：** 只用英语作为新语言；Oxford 问的是回酒店的路，不是回医院。

## R11 - 母亲：种植、帮助邻居与环保

**覆盖：** PE03、PE07、PE09

**中文记忆链：** 母亲退休后在花园种花和薄荷 - 浇水、施肥、移植 - 让环境更好，也能放松和打发退休时间 - 邻居忙或外出时帮助照看植物、猫狗，给狗喂食和遛狗 - 教自己不要破坏公共草地和花朵 - 相似商品中优先选择可回收包装，例如牛奶包装。

**一分钟关键词：**

> mother → garden → flowers + mint → water / fertilise / transplant → relax in retirement → neighbours → plants / dogs / cats → feed / walk → public flowers → recyclable packaging
